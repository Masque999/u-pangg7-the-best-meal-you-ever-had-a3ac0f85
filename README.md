# The Best Meal You Ever Had

One sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal I ever had.

Entry: `The best meal I ever had was a street taco at 2 a.m., eaten standing up, juice running down my arm, and I have chased that exact happiness in every restaurant since.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
